What you need to qualify

Requirements to start driving under our company.

  • Valid Class A CDL with 2+ years experience
  • Clean driving record (MVR), no major violations
  • Passing DOT drug test and active Clearinghouse record
  • Clean DOT annual truck & trailer inspection (last 30 days)
  • Bobtail / non-trucking liability insurance
  • ELD compatible equipment or smart mobile device

Straight answers

What drivers ask us most before joining.

Are there long-term forced contracts?

No. We earn your business every week. There are no lock-in contracts; you can leave with proper notice.

Do you force dispatch loads?

Never. You have 100% right of refusal on any load. You tell us where you want to drive, your home time needs, and minimum rate.

How fast do I get paid after completing a load?

Settlements are processed weekly every Friday by direct ACH deposit once your clean BOL/POD copies are submitted.

Can I use my own fuel card?

Yes. Use your own cards, or use our company fuel discount program to save up to $0.50/gal at major fuel stops nationwide.
